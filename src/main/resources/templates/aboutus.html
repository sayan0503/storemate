About StoreMate

StoreMate is a simple and efficient store management platform designed to help businesses manage their inventory, sales and financial performance from one convenient place.

What is StoreMate?

Managing products, keeping track of stock and monitoring sales can become difficult as a store grows. StoreMate brings these everyday tasks together into a single platform, making it easier to keep track of what is happening in your store.


With StoreMate, users can create and manage multiple stores, maintain product information, record sales and view useful reports about their business performance.

What StoreMate Offers

Inventory Management

Keep track of products, stock quantities, purchase prices and selling prices in one place.

Sales Management

Record sales and automatically update inventory when products are sold.

Business Reports

View revenue, profit and top-selling products to better understand store performance.

Multiple Stores

Manage multiple stores from the same account while keeping their data organized separately.

Product Tracking

Easily identify products and monitor their available stock as sales take place.

Simple Interface

StoreMate focuses on keeping store management straightforward without unnecessary complexity.

Our Mission

Our goal is to make store management simpler, more organized and easier to understand. StoreMate is built around the idea that useful business information should be accessible without making everyday store operations complicated.
